Ans 2(a):
BFS explores neighbor nodes level by level using a Queue (FIFO). Time complexity is O(V + E) where V is vertices and E is edges.
Rubric: Queue/FIFO mechanism: 3 marks; O(V+E) complexity: 2 marks.

Ans 2(b):
BFS uses a Queue and requires O(V) space proportional to the maximum width of the graph. DFS uses a Stack (or call stack) and requires O(h) space proportional to tree/graph depth.
Rubric: Queue vs Stack: 2.5 marks; Space complexity comparison: 2.5 marks.
