Answer 1:
In B-Trees, data pointers are stored in both internal and leaf nodes. In B+ Trees, data pointers are stored only in leaf nodes, and leaf nodes are linked as a linked list for fast sequential range queries.
Rubric: B-Tree internal data: 3 marks; B+ leaf chaining & range queries: 3 marks.

Answer 2:
AVL rotations rebalance trees with height difference > 1. LL rotation is a single right rotation. LR rotation is a left rotation on left child followed by right rotation on root.
Rubric: LL rotation: 2 marks; LR rotation: 2 marks.
