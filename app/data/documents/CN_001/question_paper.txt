Q1. [6 Marks]
A LAN has a rated bandwidth of 10 Mbps. During a measurement interval, the network was
observed to carry 12,000 frames per minute, where each frame is 10,000 bits long.
(a) Calculate the total number of bits transmitted per minute. [2]
(b) Compute the effective throughput of the LAN in bps and in Mbps. [2]
(c) Explain why the measured throughput is lower than the rated bandwidth of 10 Mbps. [2]
Q2. [7 Marks]

CB84 000D 001C 001C
(a) Separate the header into its hexadecimal byte pairs and identify the four UDP header
fields. [2]
(b) Determine the source port, destination port, UDP length and checksum values (give
decimal values where applicable). [3]
(c) Given that the UDP header is 8 bytes long, calculate the size of the UDP data. [1]
(d) State the byte order used for port fields and explain what the Length field represents. [1]
